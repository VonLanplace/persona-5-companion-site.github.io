TV Quiz

The TV quiz show only appears on certain dates, and the questions are easy to miss if you are not watching television that evening. This table lists every broadcast and its correct answer.

While staying in Cafe LeBlanc, the Protagonist can approach the nearest television and participate in a TV quiz program. If the answer is correct, the Protagonist will receive an additional Knowledge +1 social stat boost.

The TV quiz show occurs at specific dates and so the player must watch out for it in the game. The Protagonist can also use the television he purchased from the Yongen-jaya Second-hand Shop to answer a TV quiz.
