Movies

The movies are a reliable way to turn one afternoon or evening into social stat points, and a first viewing is worth more than a repeat. Each theater keeps its own schedule, so this guide lists what is showing where and when.

You can watch movies in theaters found in Shibuya, Shinjuki, and Yongen-Jaya to increase your social stats. The theaters are open during the day, after school, and in the evening.

Seeing a movie for the first time grants you +3 in a specific social stat. Meanwhile, watching a movie you have already seen only grants a +1 increase. An exception to this is when watching a movie during special events with one of your friends. Doing grants you the normal +3 increase in a social stat, regardless of having watched it before or not.

Similar to DVDs, after reading the book Cinema Treasures, the social stat increases gained is raised by one point.
