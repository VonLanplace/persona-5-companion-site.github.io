Books

Reading is one of the cheapest ways to raise a social stat: most books take only one or two free time slots, and several unlock new activities. This list covers every book in Persona 5 Royal, split into the ones you find for free and the ones you buy, with the effect of each title.

Similar to in Persona 4, reading books in Persona 5 and Persona 5 Royal will also grant the Protagonist additional social stat boost in. Certain books may also provide a boost to specific activities.
