Billiards

Penguin Sniper in Kichijoji runs a billiards table that costs 800 yen per session. The mini-game is automatic, but raising your Technical Rank requires Proficiency, specific books and repeated visits.

The billiards mini-game is a side activity in Persona 5 Royal that can be accessed at the Penguin Sniper bar in Kichijoji. One session costs 800 yen.

Billiards Basic Rules and Mechanics

Shots are not made manually and each session plays out like an event only. Playing billiards increases your Technical Rank and successfully performing a trick shot will increase the power of Technical Attacks in battle. In addition, it increases a random social stat of the Protagonist except for Kindness. Saving your game before playing Billiards is essential if you want to boost a specific Social Stat.

How to unlock Billiard Tricks

When you first start playing billiards, you don’t have access to tricks. Unlocking tricks requires playing billiards, building a Proficiency level, and brushing up on books. The first book can be bought but the other one requires performing some steps.

After playing for the first time, you will be able to purchase and read the “Billiard Techniques” book from the Next Ace sports shop at the Shibuya Underground Mall. This raises your Technical Rank to Level 1.

To raise your Technical Rank from Level 2 to Level 3, you must purchase and read “Sharp Cue” from the Next Ace sports shop again. After reaching Technical Rank 3, you will automatically acquire Billiard Magician. Read it and raise max out your Proficiency Social Stat to raise your Technical Rank to Level 4.
