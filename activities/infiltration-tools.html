Infiltration Tools

Crafting tools is the main repeatable source of Proficiency, and the materials come from two places: loot dropped by Shadows and stock sold in specific shops. The tables below list both sources for every material.

Infiltration Tool Materials

Materials are important ingredients used to craft infiltration tools. You can obtain them by looting shadows in palaces and mementos. Some materials are easier to obtain than others. You can also visit shops to purchase some of the more common drops in limited quantities. More often than not, you’re better off defeating shadows to craft a large supply of tools.

Below is a list of the shadows that drop materials for crafting infiltration tools. Locations of shops can be found under each table.
